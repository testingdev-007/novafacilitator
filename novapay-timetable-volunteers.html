Wednesday 21st October

The full-day NovaPay timetable with an extra Volunteer Focus column, so you know what to concentrate on during each timeslot.

📅 Wednesday 21st October

Timetable

Here is the day in order, with what volunteers should focus on in each slot.

Time
Activity
Volunteer Focus
  1. 09:00
    Arrival and Registration
    Volunteer FocusWelcome schools, escort groups, check attendance, starter activities.
  2. 09:30
    Welcome and Introduction
    Volunteer FocusMark and Jo introduce the session.
  3. 09:45
    Website Makeover - NovaPay Edition
    Volunteer FocusIn groups, guided by the developers, students will be shown the GitHub environment and immediately start with the task of fixing bugs, design issues, and improving the page. Page changes will need to be saved and pushed and then the other browser tab refreshed to reflect the iterative changes. There is a second task for students that easily complete the first to the satisfaction of the developers.
  4. 10:30
    Break
    Volunteer FocusKeep students with their school representatives.
  5. 10:40
    Effective Prompting & Personas
    Volunteer FocusMicrosoft to work with groups to discuss effective prompting, developers to introduce concept of personas.
  6. 11:00
    Feature Creative Collision
    Volunteer FocusGroups pool ideas for suggestions for their own NovaPay webpage / feature, with developers on hand to flesh ideas out. May need notepads and pens to help the process. Groups must have at least 1 fully formed idea and 1 suggested persona.
  7. 11:10
    Feature Build
    Volunteer FocusGroups begin using Copilot in GitHub to start the iterative process. Groups meet their Product Manager who will discuss and help refine the idea and approve the chosen persona(s). The Product Managers will also check on progress before the end of this session. Groups continue to build and iterate.
  8. 12:10
    Lunch
    Volunteer FocusEncourage students to explore accessibility workstations. Product Managers will meet in private and decide on 1 additional aspect or feature or change of focus and 1 additional persona whose feedback needs taken into account.
  9. 12:40
    Feature Build, Test, & Refine
    Volunteer FocusHelp teams restart promptly. They need to incorporate at least 1 aspect of accessibility into their final design(s) and features and test them thoroughly. They will also have a visit from the Product Manager who will give each team their "surprise" feature and persona. Groups will need to decide who will be talking through their output in the Show and Tell. More able groups can tackle a second feature with the help of the developer. Final designs need to be thoroughly tested and have the personas review. Persona review must form part of the Show and Tell.
  10. 13:50
    Show & Tell
    Volunteer FocusTeams demonstrate their final designs and discuss with audience. Max 10 mins per team. Microsoft then decide the "winning team" and hand out certificates.
  11. 14:20
    Wrap Up
    Volunteer FocusPost-it note feedback of the day as a whole, final comments from Jo and Mark.
  12. 14:30
    Departure
    Volunteer FocusEscort schools to the exit and collect visitor passes.
